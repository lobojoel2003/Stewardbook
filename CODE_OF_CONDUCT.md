# Code of Conduct

Stewardbook exists to help working people stand up for each other. We expect the same spirit in this project.

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or union
membership and role.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith — stewards, organizers and developers bring different expertise.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Protect people's privacy: never share real grievance details or member information.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Stewardbook/security/advisories/new) (it reaches the
maintainer confidentially) or by contacting them through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
contributions and may ban contributors who don't follow this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
