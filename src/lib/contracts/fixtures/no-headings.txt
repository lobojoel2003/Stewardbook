Grievances. An employee who believes the agreement has been violated must file a written grievance with the shop foreman no later than 5 business days after the incident. The foreman will reply within 2 business days. If unsatisfied, the steward may appeal to the plant manager within three (3) business days of the reply, and the plant manager must respond within five (5) business days. Days off, vacation and sick leave are covered elsewhere.
