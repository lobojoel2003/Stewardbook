AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE TOWN OF MAPLE RIDGE AND MAPLE RIDGE PUBLIC EMPLOYEES ASSOCIATION

ARTICLE I
RECOGNITION
The Town recognizes the Association as the exclusive representative of all non-supervisory employees of the Department of Public Works.

ARTICLE II
HOLIDAYS
Employees shall be entitled to the following paid holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King, Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Good Friday, Memorial Day, the Fourth of July, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans' Day, Thanksgiving Day and the Friday following Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Any holiday which falls on a Sunday shall be observed on the following Monday.

ARTICLE III
GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
A. All days referred to in this Article are calendar days.
B. STEP ONE: The employee or the Association shall discuss the grievance with the immediate supervisor within 14 days of its occurrence. The supervisor shall respond orally within 3 days.
C. STEP TWO: If the grievance is not resolved, it shall be reduced to writing and submitted to the Superintendent of Public Works within 7 days of the supervisor's response. A written decision shall be rendered by the Superintendent within 10 days of receipt.
D. STEP THREE: Within 10 days after receiving the Superintendent's decision, the Association may submit the grievance to the Town Manager. The Town Manager shall hold a hearing within fifteen days and issue a decision within 10 working days after the hearing.
E. Grievances concerning payroll errors may be submitted within forty-eight (48) hours of payday.

ARTICLE IV
ARBITRATION
If the Association is not satisfied with the decision at Step Three, it may request arbitration within twenty-one (21) days of the Town Manager's decision.
