COLLECTIVE BARGAINING AGREEMENT

between

NORTHGATE DISTRIBUTION COMPANY
(the "Employer")

and

ALLIED WAREHOUSE WORKERS, LOCAL 512
(the "Union")

July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2028

This is a fictional sample agreement shipped with Stewardbook for demonstration and testing.
It does not describe any real employer or union.


ARTICLE 1 – RECOGNITION

Section 1.1 The Employer recognizes the Union as the sole and exclusive bargaining representative
for all full-time and regular part-time warehouse associates, forklift operators, shipping and
receiving clerks, and maintenance technicians employed at the Northgate distribution center,
excluding supervisors, guards and office clerical employees.


ARTICLE 2 – MANAGEMENT RIGHTS

Section 2.1 Except as expressly limited by this Agreement, the Employer retains the right to
direct the workforce, to determine schedules and methods of operation, and to hire, promote,
transfer and assign employees.


ARTICLE 3 – STEWARDS

Section 3.1 The Union may designate one (1) steward and one (1) alternate for each shift and
department. The Union shall notify the Employer in writing of the names of its stewards.

Section 3.2 Stewards shall be permitted reasonable paid time during working hours to investigate
and present grievances, after notifying their supervisor.


ARTICLE 4 – NON-DISCRIMINATION

Section 4.1 Neither the Employer nor the Union shall discriminate against any employee on the
basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age,
disability, veteran status or Union activity.


ARTICLE 5 – HOURS OF WORK

Section 5.1 The normal work week shall consist of five (5) consecutive eight (8) hour days,
Monday through Friday.

Section 5.2 Each employee shall receive a paid fifteen (15) minute rest period in each half of a
shift and an unpaid thirty (30) minute meal period.

Section 5.3 Schedule changes shall be posted at least fourteen (14) calendar days in advance,
except in an emergency.


ARTICLE 6 – OVERTIME

Section 6.1 Time and one-half (1.5x) the regular rate shall be paid for all hours worked in
excess of eight (8) in a day or forty (40) in a week.

Section 6.2 Overtime shall be offered by seniority on a rotating basis within each department
and classification. An employee who is improperly passed over for overtime shall be paid for the
hours missed at the overtime rate.

Section 6.3 Except in an emergency, mandatory overtime shall not exceed ten (10) hours in any
work week, and employees shall be given at least two (2) hours' notice.


ARTICLE 7 – SENIORITY

Section 7.1 Seniority is the length of continuous service with the Employer since the most
recent date of hire.

Section 7.2 Layoffs shall be made in reverse order of seniority within a classification, and
recalls in order of seniority. Employees shall receive at least ten (10) working days' notice of
layoff.


ARTICLE 8 – DISCIPLINE AND DISCHARGE

Section 8.1 No employee shall be disciplined or discharged except for just cause. Discipline
shall be corrective and progressive: verbal warning, written warning, suspension, discharge.

Section 8.2 An employee has the right to Union representation at any investigatory interview
that the employee reasonably believes may lead to discipline. The Employer shall honor the
employee's request and shall allow a reasonable delay for a steward to attend.

Section 8.3 The Employer shall give the employee and the Union written notice of any discipline
within five (5) working days of the date the Employer knew of the conduct.

Section 8.4 Written warnings shall be removed from an employee's file after twelve (12) months
without further discipline for a similar offense.


ARTICLE 9 – GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

Section 9.1 Definition. A grievance is any dispute between the Employer and the Union or an
employee concerning the interpretation, application or claimed violation of this Agreement.

Section 9.2 Time Limits. For the purposes of this Article and Article 10, "days" means working
days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and the holidays listed in Article 11. The time limits in this
Article may be extended only by mutual agreement in writing.

Section 9.3 Step 1. The Union shall present the grievance in writing to the employee's immediate
supervisor within ten (10) working days after the employee knew or reasonably should have known
of the event giving rise to the grievance. The supervisor shall give the Union a written answer
within five (5) working days after receipt of the grievance.

Section 9.4 Step 2. If the grievance is not settled at Step 1, the Union may appeal it in
writing to the Operations Manager within seven (7) working days after receipt of the Step 1
answer. The Operations Manager shall meet with the steward and the grievant within five (5)
working days of the appeal and shall answer in writing within ten (10) working days after the
meeting.

Section 9.5 Step 3. If the grievance is not resolved at Step 2, the Union may appeal it in
writing to the Director of Human Resources within ten (10) working days after receipt of the
Step 2 answer. The Director shall answer in writing within fifteen (15) working days of receipt
of the appeal.

Section 9.6 Failure to Answer. If the Employer fails to answer a grievance within the time
limits, the Union may advance the grievance to the next step. A grievance not appealed within the
time limits shall be considered settled on the basis of the last answer.

Section 9.7 Discharge and suspension grievances may be filed directly at Step 2.


ARTICLE 10 – ARBITRATION

Section 10.1 If the grievance is not resolved at Step 3, the Union may refer it to arbitration by
written notice to the Employer within thirty (30) calendar days after receipt of the Step 3
answer.

Section 10.2 The parties shall select an arbitrator from a panel of seven (7) names. The
arbitrator shall issue a written decision within thirty (30) calendar days after the close of the
hearing. The decision shall be final and binding.

Section 10.3 The arbitrator shall have no authority to add to, subtract from or modify this
Agreement. The fees of the arbitrator shall be shared equally.


ARTICLE 11 – HOLIDAYS

Section 11.1 The following shall be paid holidays:

New Year's Day
Martin Luther King Jr. Day
Memorial Day
Juneteenth
Independence Day
Labor Day
Thanksgiving Day
The Day after Thanksgiving
Christmas Eve
Christmas Day

Section 11.2 When a holiday falls on a Saturday, it shall be observed on the preceding Friday.
When a holiday falls on a Sunday, it shall be observed on the following Monday.

Section 11.3 Employees required to work on a holiday shall be paid at double time in addition to
holiday pay.


ARTICLE 12 – VACATIONS

Section 12.1 Employees shall earn vacation according to the following schedule: after one (1)
year, ten (10) days; after five (5) years, fifteen (15) days; after ten (10) years, twenty (20)
days.

Section 12.2 Vacation requests submitted by March 1 shall be granted by seniority.


ARTICLE 13 – HEALTH AND SAFETY

Section 13.1 The Employer shall provide a safe and healthful workplace and all required personal
protective equipment at no cost to employees.

Section 13.2 No employee shall be required to operate equipment the employee reasonably believes
to be unsafe. A joint labor-management safety committee shall meet monthly.

Section 13.3 Forklifts and powered equipment shall be inspected at the start of every shift.
Heat illness prevention measures, including water, shade and rest, shall be provided whenever the
warehouse temperature exceeds eighty (80) degrees.


ARTICLE 14 – JOB POSTING AND BIDDING

Section 14.1 Vacancies shall be posted for five (5) working days. The senior qualified bidder
shall be awarded the position.

Section 14.2 The successful bidder shall have a trial period of thirty (30) calendar days and may
return to the former position during that period.


ARTICLE 15 – DURATION

Section 15.1 This Agreement shall be effective July 1, 2025 and shall remain in effect through
June 30, 2028.
